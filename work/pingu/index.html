Project Statement

Pingu is a platform for child development that make education fun, promote healthy habits, maintaining an active lifestyle with technology integration.

It is integrated also with a mobile app for parents, helping to track their child’s development, involve themselves in and connect with other parents.

Smartwatch is guided by a penguin character, making children identify themselves with it.

Research Insights

01

Facilitating Safe Social Interaction

“Community” should feature hobby groups, shared activities and event creation, enabling children to connect and interact safely under parental oversight.

02

Holistic Health Tracking

Implementing comprehensive monitoring — sleep, activity, hydration, heart rate and mood — to offer a complete child health profile.

03

Structure & Reinforcement

Providing structured tasks via the To-Do list for habit development, reinforced by My Pingu’s expressive feedback.

04

Gamification and Character

Ensuring gamification — character and badges — is meaningful and educative, and makes children identify themselves with the character.

Mobile App Features

01

To-Do

Enables parents to view and add activities for their child across school, lifestyle, and hobby categories.

02

Health

Enables parents to create a health profile and monitor their child's activity, sleep, and hydration levels.

03

Community

Allows parents and children to connect with others and discover or announce events and activities they can join together.

04

Sports

Enables activity tracking while also helping users discover nearby sports events and activities.

05

Art

Brings together the child's interests, activities, and creative work in a personalized collection.

Smartwatch App Features

Main Menu

Provides quick access to all smartwatch features and subfunctions.

My Pingu

Provides emotion-based feedback through the Pingu character, helping children recognize and reflect on their feelings.

To-Do

Allows children to access their daily tasks, categorized into school, lifestyle, and hobbies.

Health

Tracks health data including sleep, exercise, and hydration, presenting it to children through a gamified experience.

Sports

Encourages activities such as dancing, swimming, running, and yoga through motivating gamified experiences.

Art & DIY

Offers creative art and making activities through playful, gamified challenges that motivate children to create.

Community

Allows children to easily view their friends and shared activities.
