Project Goals & Brief

01

Digital collaboration

Design a digital solution that helps remote-working teams collaborate, communicate and engage with one another in a more meaningful way.

02

Beyond productivity

The brief asked how digital experiences could support the human side of distributed work, creating space for the informal interactions that help teams feel connected.

TrustBelongingCreativityEveryday team interaction
02

Problem Statement

Remote workers lose the natural office interactions that build sustainable bonds and team visibility. They need low-pressure, spontaneous ways to socialize from onboarding through daily work and after-hours moments.

Research Overview

Multiple research streams were combined to understand remote-work behavior, existing products and the moments where social connection breaks down.

01

Desk Research

  • Literature Review
  • Industry Benchmarking
  • Workplace pattern mapping
02

Survey Overviews

  • Conducted a survey with 28 participants.
  • Google Forms was the tool used.
03

Interview Overviews

  • Conducted semi-structured interviews with 5 Commencis designers from different projects.

Benchmark Insights

01

Low Regular Usage Rate

The rates of regular adoption and daily workflow usage by employees remain quite low.

02

Familiar Interfaces

Social media feeds (Blink, Workvivo) or game-like maps (Gather.town) are used to speed up adaptation.

03

Productivity Based

The majority of the benchmarks are not going beyond productivity, similar to Teams and Slack.

05

Research Insights

The findings pointed toward small, spontaneous and interest-led interactions rather than more crowded, formal meetings.

78%

missed spontaneous water-cooler interactions the most.

58%

preferred micro-gatherings of two or three people over crowded events.

66%

struggled with losing informal touchpoints with other teams.

Interest-driven connection

Cold interactions fail. Common hobbies, topics and automated facilitation can make connection feel more organic.

Lack of adaptation

Without structured re-engagement, a divide can persist between original teams and new hires.

UI Design System

10

Final Experience — Mobile App

The mobile experience brings everyday connection into four clear areas: Home, Hub, Games and Social.

01

Home

A pulse section welcomes users with news and onboarding, followed by the breakout zone where people get into rooms through Teams.

02

Hub

Hub is divided into two segments where feed shows company news and announcements. Sources part is enhanced with content shared by company and quick links.

03

Games

Games part enhances the break-time and created collaborations and interactions between people with leaderboards, and commonly created challenges etc.

04

Social

Social includes the company communities where people can meet, gather, arrange events together. Commencers ask part also creates a free environment where people can share randomly.

11

Desktop App & Vibe Coding

The desktop experience carries communities, quick chats and shared team moments into the workday. A Coffee Chat flow was then developed through vibe coding and prototyping.
