Literature Research

What is “New Generation” Mobile Banking?

The new generation mobile banking concept shifts applications from static transaction hubs to proactive, hyper-personalized financial companions.

How is Gen Z inspiring a reinvention of banking?

Gen Z is demanding personalization, transparency and digital-first solutions when it comes to managing their money.

03

Key Features of New-Generation Banking

01

AI-Driven Personalization

Machine learning analyzes spending habits to automate savings, forecast upcoming bills and proactively flag anomalies, reducing the need for manual budgeting.

02

Hyper-Intuitive UI

Dynamic, context-aware interfaces prioritize relevant information and frequently needed actions.

03

Financial Super Apps

Modern banking applications combine traditional banking with services such as investments, travel, insurance and digital-wallet utilities.

04

Embedded Social Finance

Person-to-person transfers, mobile-wallet integration, connected bank accounts and frictionless international transfers create a more integrated financial ecosystem.

05

Frictionless Onboarding

Digital onboarding and biometric authentication allow users to access banking services with fewer procedural barriers.

User Research

7

Interview Participants

01

Customization

Users valued being able to arrange their most frequently used actions.

02

QR Actions

QR-based actions were considered useful and worth making more accessible.

03

Investment

Users valued being able to follow investments, stocks and crypto within a single application.

04

Split & Pay

Easy shared payments between friends were considered useful.

05

Less Friction

Users preferred shorter, simpler and easier-to-scan interactions.

06

Easy Authentication

Face ID, ID scanning and video-based authentication were preferred over repeated traditional passcodes.

07

Modern UI

Participants appreciated the modern and clean interfaces of applications such as Midas and Revolut.

  • Stocks

  • Commodities

Finances & Trust AI

The Finances experience combines financial visibility, goal tracking and AI-assisted support to help users understand and manage their financial life from a single place.

  • Saving Milestones
  • Trust AI

International Transfer
